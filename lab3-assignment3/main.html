Stanford: 4 on-campus beverages you should be drinking

Victoria Kirst
Last updated Apr 7, 2017

1. Italian soda at Bytes

Bytes is one of the most popular campus spots for grabbing a quick drink between classes. Their Italian sodas are made with sparkling water and fruit syrup, customizable with dozens of unique flavor combinations. The cozy seating area makes it a great place to catch up on reading or hang out with friends.

2. Bubble tea at Pop Tea Bar

Pop Tea Bar offers an extensive menu of authentic bubble teas with various milk and fruit tea bases. You can choose your sweetness level, ice level, and toppings like tapioca pearls or lychee jelly. It's the ultimate refreshing treat on a warm sunny afternoon walking across campus.

3. Matcha latte at CoHo

The Coffee House (CoHo) serves premium ceremonial-grade matcha lattes whisked to perfection. Known for its earthy flavor and smooth texture, it provides a sustained energy boost without the jittery crash associated with traditional coffee drinks.

4. Vanilla Bean Latte

A classic favorite featuring rich espresso combined with steamed milk and sweet vanilla bean syrup. Recommendation: Vanilla Bean Latte is the perfect comforting companion for early morning study sessions at the library.
